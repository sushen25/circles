What we measure

app_nudge_shown / dismissed / tapped by moment · account_claimed by moment · app_first_open_linked · share of installed members whose first touch was a guest link · guest → new circle within 30 days

What we never do

An install wall. An “open in app” banner on the join or availability screens. A prompt inside an operational email. A second nudge in the same session. Any feature of the core loop held back from the web.

Why in this order

The guest has to experience a confirmed meetup before anything is asked (moat research §6.3). Saving a place is a smaller ask than installing and unlocks organising, so it comes first wherever the person is about to organise. The app is pitched on the four things a browser can't do.
