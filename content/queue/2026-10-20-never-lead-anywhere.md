> Someone on r/boardgames put it better than I have in months of trying: group chats never lead anywhere because nobody feels directly called upon.
>
> I'd been reading it as a motivation problem. Four people say yes and the plan still dies, so surely they didn't mean it. Rereading our own chats, that's wrong. They meant it. Each of them was waiting for someone to name a night, and "someone" was never a person.
>
> What I'm building in Melbourne is mostly a way to make the ask land on each person without one friend having to chase the other three. One link in the chat, everyone marks the times they'd actually be up for, and the organiser locks one in. No app, no account.
