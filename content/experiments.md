# Experiments

One row per hook family per fortnight. One piece a day (`/daily`), rotating
through the live families; everything else waits. Shapes (from the skit
skill) rotate within a family, so a fortnight compares shapes as well as
families. Day 30: kill the two worst, double the best.

Metric: saves + shares per 1,000 views on TikTok (hand-entered weekly), link
taps on wenna.app by the `?from=` tag every published link carries (`lib/links.ts`), and replies that quote the hook
back. Likes are not a metric. "Plans shared" is plans shared to a chat by organisers
whose circle carries this post's tag (Wenna's founder screen reads the funnel
by `source_ref` once circles SUS-240 lands); it is the only column that decides
whether content is a channel. Until then, "Link taps" is what the platform
reports and "Plans shared" stays blank rather than guessed.
